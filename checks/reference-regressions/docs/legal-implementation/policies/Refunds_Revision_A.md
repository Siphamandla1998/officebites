## 1. Scope and legal rights
This policy applies to orders made through OfficeBites, an online marketplace where the identified independent vendor ordinarily supplies the food. OfficeBites facilitates ordering, certain payments, customer support and, where applicable, delivery arrangements. OfficeBites coordinates complaints and refunds processed through the platform. Responsibility for a particular problem depends on the actual transaction and applicable law. Nothing here excludes or restricts non-excludable rights under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (CPA), the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 (ECTA) or other applicable law.

## 2. Before preparation starts
For a customer-requested cancellation made before genuine meal preparation begins, OfficeBites ordinarily arranges a full refund of amounts paid for the cancelled order, subject to applicable law. A vendor or platform status marked “preparing” is not by itself proof that preparation has started or that costs were incurred. Where the CPA section 17 applies to an advance order or booking, any cancellation charge must meet its statutory reasonableness requirements and exceptions. No section 17 cancellation fee may be charged where the booking cannot be honoured due to the death or hospitalisation of the intended beneficiary, in the circumstances covered by that provision.

## 3. After preparation begins
Where meal preparation has actually started, a customer-requested change-of-mind cancellation may incur only a reasonable, evidenced charge if permitted by applicable law and disclosed before the order was accepted. We consider the order's circumstances and amounts reasonably incurred or avoidable. There is no automatic fixed percentage or blanket “no refunds after preparation” rule. Once food is ready or dispatched, cancellation for change of mind may be limited as lawfully permitted, but quality, non-delivery, incorrect-order and other statutory remedies remain available.

## 4. Wrong, missing, damaged, unsafe or poor-quality food
Customers should report wrong or missing items, spoiled, contaminated or unsafe food, materially inaccurate descriptions, damaged packaging or suspected allergens promptly. Depending on evidence and applicable law, available remedies may include replacement, refund of affected items, refund of appropriate associated delivery charges, or a full refund. Serious food safety concerns are escalated promptly. Customers may retain packaging or photographs where safe and reasonable; they should not be required to keep or consume unsafe food simply to prove a claim. A statutory quality claim is not barred solely because food is perishable or consumed. The scope and practical application of CPA section 56 and other remedies to the particular food transaction should be confirmed through legal review.

## 5. Late, failed or unavailable delivery
The delivery date, service window and fee accepted at checkout form the starting point for reviewing performance. Where an order is materially late, never delivered or unavailable, OfficeBites coordinates an appropriate response based on the actual circumstances and applicable law. We do not automatically refuse remedies because a delivery provider, rather than the vendor, caused the issue. A refund may cover undelivered food, affected delivery charges or another lawful adjustment. We will not impose a replacement order without customer agreement.

## 6. Reporting a problem
Customers can use **Help/Support** or available in-platform order messaging, email **officebites4@gmail.com**, and WhatsApp or telephone where those channels are actually published and operational. Please provide an order reference and description of the issue; we may request proportionate supporting information. Customers are **encouraged** to report visible meal issues within **two hours after receipt** to support rapid investigation. This is **not a forfeiture deadline** and does not override statutory rights, delayed illness or facts that could not reasonably have been discovered immediately.

## 7. Acknowledgement and handling
OfficeBites targets acknowledgement within **two published support hours**. Reports submitted outside published support hours are recorded immediately where the system permits, and the target begins when support resumes. An automatic ticket-received message is not necessarily a completed human acknowledgement. Investigation and resolution periods depend on the circumstances; OfficeBites aims to keep customers reasonably informed. Urgent food safety and security issues are prioritised under applicable internal procedures and law.

## 8. Review thresholds and decision-making
OfficeBites authorises refunds processed through its platform, having considered information from the customer, vendor, relevant payment provider and delivery participant. **Requests below R200** ordinarily receive simplified documented verification. **Requests of R200 or more** receive proportionate additional review. These are **internal workflow thresholds, not limits on refund eligibility or rights**. Suspected fraud or serious safety concerns may require extra scrutiny at any amount. OfficeBites' determination is final only for its **internal processing workflow** and does not remove the customer's right to seek independent assistance or legal remedies.

## 9. Refund method and timing
An approved refund is issued using a **secure, appropriate method**, ordinarily through the original payment provider if supported, and otherwise through an appropriately verified alternative method. OfficeBites **aims to initiate** an approved refund within **five business days of approval**, or sooner where required by applicable law. Actual receipt depends on bank or provider processes; specific statutory repayment deadlines still apply. Never share your bank password, PIN or one-time password to receive a refund. Where the vendor received the original payment directly, OfficeBites coordinates and tracks the vendor or provider's refund obligation, and provides status updates where available; we do not promise unilateral access to vendor bank accounts.

## 10. Payment failures, duplicate collections and chargebacks
Where a payment is incorrectly captured, duplicated or not matched to a valid order, OfficeBites investigates using payment-provider and order records. Where money is owed back, the relevant parties coordinate prompt lawful correction. Customers should not receive the same refund twice; any overlapping chargeback and refund processes will be reconciled with the provider. The platform must not treat an unverified browser success screen as authoritative proof that a payment settled.

## 11. Discounts, delivery and fees on refunds
Promotional offers and refund amounts are reconciled against the amount actually paid and any valid platform- or vendor-funded subsidy. Internal commissions, marketing fees and vendor allocations are corrected on refunded eligible food sales under the Vendor Agreement. **Internal allocation disputes must not delay legally due customer remedies.** Customer-facing refunds are not reduced to recover OfficeBites' ordinary vendor commission unless a separate lawful customer charge was expressly agreed.

## 12. Group orders and special orders
Bulk purchases are subject to prior capacity approval where applicable, with material terms and reasonable cancellation conditions disclosed before confirmation. Special-order goods and ordinary advance bookings are not necessarily treated identically under CPA section 17. Any individually negotiated cancellation charges must remain lawful and proportionate. An employer is not liable merely because food is delivered to its workplace.

## 13. Consumer-law treatment of online food orders
ECTA's general section 44 seven-day cooling-off right has exceptions, including section 42(2)(c) for food and beverages intended for everyday consumption supplied to a customer's home or workplace, and other relevant exceptions. **These exceptions do not remove CPA quality, performance, misdescription or other applicable statutory remedies.** No blanket seven-day food-return promise or blanket exclusion of all refunds is made. Legal review must verify how each type of food, delivery or other transaction is classified.

## 14. Escalation, records and policy changes
Customers may request an internal reconsideration and pursue applicable external consumer remedies. OfficeBites maintains records of the request, its timing, evidence, decision, payment reference and resolution, subject to the **Privacy** policy. Changes to this policy apply prospectively, with appropriate notice for material customer changes; no unlawful retrospective deprivation of rights is permitted.
