# OfficeBites — Cookies

**Revision B | 24 October 2026 | WORKING DRAFT — NOT FOR PUBLICATION**

OfficeBites may use browser storage and similar technologies to make ordering and account functions work, protect sessions and assess platform performance. This description must be checked against an actual inventory of the scripts, cookies and local-storage keys used by the live application.

## Essential storage
Strictly necessary session, login, shopping-cart, checkout and security technologies may be used where lawful for the service requested. Disabling them may prevent some functions from working.

## Optional categories
If actually deployed, optional technology may support performance monitoring, analytics, saved preferences or personalisation. Advertising pixels are not part of the approved current scope. The identity of providers, purposes, storage duration and categories must be listed accurately before publication.

## Choices and changes
Where processing relies on consent, optional scripts must not load before a valid choice. Customers should be able to select categories separately, reject optional categories and change preferences later. A visually polished but non-functional banner does not satisfy this requirement. Other lawful bases and relevant legal obligations must be assessed rather than treating every cookie identically.

## Contact and privacy
For access, objections or privacy questions, see **Privacy** at /privacy or email officebites4@gmail.com. Marketing communications require their own appropriate permission or lawful basis, not inferred consent from necessary checkout functionality.

## Publication verification
Inventory actual providers and domains, cookies/local storage, retention, script gating, analytics settings, cross-border processing and user controls. Do not say a cookie preference center exists until independently tested on web and mobile.
