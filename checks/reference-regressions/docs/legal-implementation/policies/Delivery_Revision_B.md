# OfficeBites — Delivery

**Revision B | 24 October 2026 | WORKING DRAFT — NOT FOR PUBLICATION**

OfficeBites provides a marketplace where delivery may be arranged by approved vendors or by OfficeBites through approved partners. The customer should be informed of the applicable delivery method, cost, location, window and responsible provider before confirming an order.

## Service area and fees
Availability depends on the customer destination, order location, vendor capacity and an approved pricing configuration. The initial administrative reference is Eduvos, Umhlanga; this distance-pricing reference is not necessarily the vendor's actual kitchen or pickup location. Vendor-specific origin settings need OfficeBites approval. The proposed R10 outer-zone charge, 0.5 km free radius and 5 km maximum radius are not binding customer promises until verified as active for the particular order. The only amount payable is the total delivery charge disclosed and accepted at checkout.

## Operating hours and cutoff
OfficeBites publishes operating days, support hours, service windows and per-window order cutoffs. Vendor-specific exceptions require approval. A later change cannot silently vary a confirmed order; customers receive notice and lawful remedies for material disruption. Orders outside open windows are handled as scheduled orders only if the checkout expressly supports and confirms scheduling.

## Delivery obligations
Vendors prepare and package safe, accurately described food and prepare for the agreed collection time. The applicable deliverer is responsible for its own agreed delivery duties and safe handling to the extent law and contract require. OfficeBites helps coordinate concerns and investigate the chain of custody, without purporting to disclaim responsibilities that attach to its actual role.

## Delay, failed delivery, loss and damage
Customers should contact OfficeBites promptly through Help/Support where an order is missing, substantially late, incorrect, unsafe or damaged. The platform assesses preparation records, dispatch, handover, communication and delivery circumstances. Outcomes may include reasonable redelivery, replacement, refund or another lawful remedy based on the facts. A two-hour complaint guideline is not a legal forfeiture of consumer rights.

## Customer location information
Use delivery information solely for authorised fulfilment, fraud prevention and support. Avoid sharing irrelevant details with vendors and delivery parties; see **Privacy**.

## Publication verification
