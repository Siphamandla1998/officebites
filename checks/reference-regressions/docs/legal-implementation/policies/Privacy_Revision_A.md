## 1. Who we are

OfficeBites operates an online food-ordering marketplace connecting customers with independent food vendors. OFFICEBITES (PTY) LTD decides the purposes and means of processing personal information for its own marketplace, support, account and security activities. Depending on the transaction, the independent vendor and payment service provider may separately determine how they process information for their own legal obligations and services. Some service providers process information on OfficeBites' instructions.

You can contact us about privacy, access, correction, objections and complaints at **officebites4@gmail.com**, or through the available Help/Support channel. **[VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION: Information Officer identity/contact, company disclosure link and official physical address.]**

## 2. Information we process

Depending on the feature you use, we process:

- **Orders and fulfilment:** name, mobile number, selected delivery or collection location, order items, delivery instructions, nominated independent vendor, order references, status and transaction history.
- **Accounts and guest purchases:** account identifiers and access records where registered accounts are used; guest order references and information required to verify requests without creating an account.
- **Payments:** payment method selection, provider references, payment and refund status and settlement/reconciliation records. We do not ask customers to disclose online banking passwords, card PINs or one-time passwords to OfficeBites. Payment providers may collect additional information through their own secure services.
- **Messages and support:** customer-service enquiries, vendor-platform messages, complaints, refund evidence and resolution records.
- **Security and technical use:** device/browser and application logs, access and audit records, diagnostics and limited usage information necessary to operate and protect the service.
- **Optional activities, if enabled:** marketing preferences and communications, consent choices, product reviews, non-essential analytics, preference storage and personalisation. These features are **not represented as live unless verified**.

**Email, precise GPS/device location, and optional profile fields are not mandatory by default.** If a specific enabled feature requests them, the service must explain why and obtain any consent or other lawful authorisation required. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive information, including identity documents, in general support messages.

## 3. Why and on what basis we use information

We process only information reasonably needed to:

1. Take and fulfil orders, display vendor details, provide delivery/collection information and send necessary transactional messages.
2. Facilitate payment and refund status, calculate commissions and keep financial and tax records.
3. Verify customer requests, respond to complaints and prevent unauthorised access, fraudulent orders and misuse.
4. Maintain system stability, security, backups, operational logs and the integrity of accounts and order records.
5. Meet legal duties, respond to lawful requests and resolve or defend disputes.
6. Send promotional communications **only where a lawful direct-marketing basis exists and the relevant opt-out/consent conditions are met**.
7. Use optional analytics, preferences or personalisation **only when enabled with an appropriate lawful basis and the required user choice**.

The relevant justification depends on the activity and may include completing a transaction, meeting legal duties, protecting legitimate interests where lawful, or consent. Consent is not required for every essential order-processing activity; withdrawing optional consent does not undo lawful past processing or remove compulsory records.

## 4. Independent vendors and other recipients

We provide the vendor fulfilling your order with the information reasonably needed for that order, such as your name, order items, instructions and applicable delivery information. Vendors see their own orders, not other vendors' customer records. **Your telephone number or email address should not be routinely visible to vendors**; limited disclosure for a necessary fulfilment issue must be properly controlled and documented.

Vendors must not use customer data obtained through OfficeBites for their own unrelated direct marketing, unauthorised customer solicitation, or purposes inconsistent with their contractual and legal duties.

Other recipient categories may include payment providers, hosting/database operators, messaging services, mapping providers, analytics services, professional advisers and authorities where lawful. **Supabase and Vercel form part of the existing technology stack; PayFast, Ozow, WhatsApp and other service providers must be disclosed according to their actual use and roles, not merely future plans.** OfficeBites should maintain appropriate contractual safeguards with operators.

## 5. Storage and processing outside South Africa

Some data or system logs may be accessed, supported or processed outside South Africa even when a primary database is located in South Africa. OfficeBites will assess international data flows and apply the protections required by section 72 of POPIA before using relevant transfers. **[VERIFY: actual Supabase project region, Vercel services, provider/subprocessor locations and transfer basis.]**

## 6. Marketing and your choices

Order confirmations, payment updates and delivery messages are transactional and are separate from optional marketing. Promotional messages may be sent only in accordance with applicable direct-marketing requirements, including consent or a legally permitted existing-customer exception where its conditions are met. Every direct-marketing communication must identify the sender and offer an accessible way to stop future messages.

We do not grant vendors independent marketing rights over OfficeBites customer lists. Referral programmes and promotions must not expose another person's contact information without a lawful basis.

## 7. Cookies, analytics and preferences

Essential technologies may support checkout, authentication, safety and technical operation. Optional analytics, performance, preferences and personalisation may involve additional processing and must be disclosed according to actual deployed technologies and their purposes. Advertising pixels have not been approved under the current business policy.

Where optional processing depends on consent, the app should allow appropriate granular choices and later withdrawal. **[VERIFY that any consent banner genuinely blocks optional technology until the relevant legal requirements are satisfied.]** Further detail belongs in the separately linked Cookie Policy; the customer-facing page label remains **Privacy**.

## 8. How long we keep information

We retain information only for justified operational, financial, legal, security and dispute-resolution purposes, considering applicable record-retention duties and the need to restrict access. Different categories may have different periods, and some legally required records remain after a customer account is deleted. At the end of a justified period, we delete, de-identify or securely dispose of information as appropriate.

## 9. Your privacy rights

Subject to POPIA and other applicable law, you may ask whether we hold personal information about you, request access or correction, request deletion when appropriate, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent for optional activities. You may also lodge a complaint with the South African Information Regulator.

Use **officebites4@gmail.com** or an available private Help/Support channel. To protect your information, OfficeBites may ask for reasonable identity or order ownership verification. Guest customers do not need an account to exercise their privacy rights. Requests are assessed under applicable legal requirements; not every deletion request permits deletion of records we must keep by law.

Information Regulator: https://inforegulator.org.za/

## 10. Security and incident response

OfficeBites intends to use role-based access controls, appropriate encryption, controlled secret storage, administrator multi-factor authentication, audit logs, backups and periodic security review. **These are adopted requirements, not a certification or confirmation that all controls have been deployed.** Access must be limited to people and vendors with a legitimate need.

If there are reasonable grounds to believe personal information was accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, we will investigate, contain the incident and notify the Information Regulator and affected people as required by section 22 of POPIA, as soon as reasonably possible in the circumstances. Vendors and operators must promptly escalate suspected incidents affecting shared data.

## 11. Children's information

Parents or legal guardians place orders on behalf of children. OfficeBites does not offer independent ordering by minors under its initial policy. We seek to limit details about child recipients and do not authorise independent marketing or profiling of children through the marketplace.

## 12. Changes to Privacy

We may amend this notice as practices or laws change. For material customer-facing changes, our policy is ordinarily to give at least 14 calendar days' advance notice, except where urgent legal, safety or security action is reasonably necessary. Notice alone does not replace any additional consent or legal basis required for a new use of information. The current effective version and date will be displayed when an approved notice is published.
